Here's 40 years of sales experience in 60 seconds flat. You've got four seconds to sound sharp as a tack, enthusiastic as hell, and like an expert in your field. Or you're done. Every sale is the same. You take control, or you get control. Ask questions before you pitch. You can't fill a need you never found out about. Sales is the transference of emotion and that primary emotion is certainty that certainly travels through your tone, not your words. 90 % of communication is tonality and body language. Never front -load your whole presentation. You save bullet points for the objections because objections are coming and they'll all be smoke streams for uncertainty. Let me think about it means make me more certain. The client must trust three things, the product, you and your company. You miss one, no deal. Rejection is the toll booth on the road to riches. Act as if, prepare your scripts, ask for the order and remember bottled enthusiasm sells more than any word ever invented. Now go close.
